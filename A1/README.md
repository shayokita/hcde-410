# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Burke-Gilman-Trail-north-of-NE-70th-St-Bicycle-Cou/2z5v-ecg8/about_data
**Pulled:** 2026-10-16
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain

## Questions I asked
- When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest?
- Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day?
- Is there evidence people use the Burke-Gilman to commute, not just for fun?
- The counter records north vs. south separately. Are mornings mostly one direction and evenings the other? What would that tell you about who's using the trail and where they're heading?

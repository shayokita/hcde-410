Motivation & Problem:
I use TikTok every single day and I have noticed that some really short videos get millions of views and likes. I am curious whether shorter videos actually have higher engagement rates or if longer videos perform just as well. I would like to see whether video length is related to how often viewers like, comment or share a video.

Data:
I am going to use DataHive AI's TikTok Videos dataset which is a public sample of 2060 videos. It includes duration, views, likes, comments, shares and creator and posting details. I'll compare engagement rates by looking at likes, comments and shares relative to views across different video lengths. The dataset is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 for attributed, noncommercial use. Link: https://huggingface.co/datasets/datahiveai/Tiktok-Videos

Unknowns & dependencies:
The biggest limitation is that 2060 videos are only a small sample of TikTok and I don't know how they were selected. Tik tok also changes a lot so the data may not reflect current trends.

During class I got feedback about narrowing the scope of my research question since the dataset is so small compared to TikTok as a whole, as well as considering how recent the data is since TikTok trends change so quickly.
